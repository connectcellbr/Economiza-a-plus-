ANDROID
-------

   <meta-data
       android:name="com.google.android.gms.ads.APPLICATION_ID"
       android:value="ca-app-pub-4786232214626239~7650986156"/>

2) build.gradle (módulo app):

   implementation 'com.google.android.gms:play-services-ads:23.+'

3) Banner (Kotlin):

   val adView = AdView(this)
   adView.adUnitId = "ca-app-pub-4786232214626239/7263973345"
   adView.setAdSize(AdSize.BANNER)
   adView.loadAd(AdRequest.Builder().build())

4) Se o app for WebView carregando o Economiza AI:
   - Banner nativo no topo/rodapé (fora do HTML)
